# Linear model of the aircraft standing on its gear (tests/gates/diag/gear_damper.py modes)

Fastest decay rate = the most negative real eigenvalue of the standing aircraft's heave, pitch and roll motion.
A scheme is unstable roughly when (fastest decay rate) x dt exceeds its limit: Euler 2.0, AB2 1.0, AB3 0.545, AB4 0.3.
The table uses the full step map (see the file header), not that rule of thumb.
