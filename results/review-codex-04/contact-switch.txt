`c3x-ab4-fa-hold` (on-contact scheme held 0.1 s after the last contact), `c3-ab3-fa` (AB3 in the air).
'Extra' is the change of the body rates in one step minus an Euler step of the derivative that step integrated, in rad/s.
'Release kick' is the largest extra on a first step back in the air, as a fraction of one step of the last ground moment.
