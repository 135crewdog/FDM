# Linear model of the aircraft standing on its gear (tests/gates/diag/gear_damper.py modes)

Fastest decay rate = the most negative real eigenvalue of the standing aircraft's heave, pitch and roll motion.
A scheme is unstable roughly when (fastest decay rate) x dt exceeds its limit: Euler 2.0, AB2 1.0, AB3 0.545, AB4 0.3.

# Hard landings against the gear damping scale

Native JSBSim: native JSBSim 1.3.3 [patched] module sha256 04b0929860cdfa9a patches: 0001-integrator-aware-friction.patch c8abb500ee33, 0002-implicit-midpoint-body-rates.patch b6081c13df3b, 0003-body-rate-scheme-on-contact.patch 780a6e00946a
G2c's forward hard landing, runs 0 to 10 of gates-04.json, on copies of the aircraft whose ground-contact dampers are
multiplied by a scale. Each cell: runs that stay finite and without runaway, of 11.
If stiff dampers were the cause of the failures, softer dampers (scale < 1) would cure them and stiffer ones
would make them worse, and the scale that fails would move in proportion to the step rate.
